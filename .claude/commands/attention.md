---
description: Everything that wants a decision today, worst first: rules breached, who is behind on fees, boats overstaying the hardstand, licences ending, free berths someone is waiting for, papers about to lapse.
---

1. Run `npm run marina -- attention`.
2. Read it in order:
   - **Rules** first. Each line is a breach of a rule in `docs/compliance.md`: lapsed insurance, cover under the minimum, a shore power check out of date, a lift used or booked past its certificate, an overseas arrival with no biofouling evidence, an unsigned licence, a boat too long for its berth, someone living aboard without approval. Say which boat, and the one thing that clears it.
   - **Money.** Berth holders more than 30 days behind, and finished yard jobs not yet invoiced.
   - **Yard.** Boats past their agreed launch date. The hardstand is charging, so the owner should hear today.
   - **Berths.** Licences ending in 30 days with no renewal, visitors past their departure date, and free spaces that fit someone on the waiting list.
   - **Coming up.** Insurance, electrical checks and lift certificates ending inside 30 days.
3. For each item give the action and who owns it (office, yard, harbourmaster).
4. Anything that needs a letter or email is drafted to `drafts/`, never sent.

If the operator asks "what should I do today", pick the top three and say why those three.
