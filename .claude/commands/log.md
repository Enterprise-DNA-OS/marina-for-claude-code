---
description: Record a note against a boat or a person: a call, a promise to pay, something the yard noticed.
---

`npm run marina -- log <vessel or customer> "what happened" [--by=Office]`

Write the note in plain words with any date or amount in it ("Agreed to pay $1,242 by 15 Nov"). Notes show on the vessel and customer cards and are read before anything is drafted to that person.
